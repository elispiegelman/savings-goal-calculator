The basics

How this is calculated

We estimate your growth by adding interest to your balance each month, using one-twelfth of the annual return. Then we add that month’s contribution. Because each month’s interest stays in your account, it can earn interest too—that’s compound interest. This estimate assumes your return stays the same and doesn’t account for taxes, fees, or changes in investment value.
